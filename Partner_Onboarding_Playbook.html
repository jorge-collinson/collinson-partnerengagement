How to use this playbook

This playbook covers the complete journey from contract signature to a partner processing their first transactions and passing the 30-day health check. It is written for partnership managers. Partner operations, finance, and product teams will also use it as a reference for their responsibilities and handoff points.

Each stage shows two paths: the standard path (every partner) and the pre-book path (additional steps when the partner is offering pre-book with pre-agreed availability). Use the tabs at each stage to switch between them.

Reading order
If you are onboarding a partner for the first time, read the full playbook once from top to bottom. After that, use the sidebar navigation to jump to the stage you need.

What the Pre-Book Onboarding Form captures

  • Outlet details: name, IATA code, country, time zone (standard format, e.g. GMT+2, EST, SGT), notification email
  • Availability schedule: days and time periods in 30-minute blocks, capacity per block, seasonal changes, blackout dates

  • Partnership Manager sign-off: your name, outlet code, expected launch date

Onboarding

Partner Ops + Finance
Duration

Timeline note
The 21-day timeline accounts for customs clearance, translation turnaround (up to 10 business days), and listing approval processes. Complex partners (airlines with 40+ lounges, operators with 10+ locations) may require extended timelines. Partner Ops will communicate revised timelines when needed.

Phase 1: Enablement (days 1 to 14)

Day 1: Case validation
Partner Ops receives Salesforce case, validates information completeness, confirms green to proceed or pauses for missing data.
Days 2 to 5: System configuration and kit dispatch
Partner Ops configures PPBO, Product Catalogue, Lounge Gateway system, and device registration. Kit dispatch starts as soon as system setup is complete to avoid customs delays (subject to number of devices required).
Days 3 to 10: Content and listing creation
Content team creates and translates the lounge listing using partner-provided materials. Translation can take up to 10 business days. Content team sends listing to the Partnership Manager with comments/questions or for final approval with partner. Once addressed and approved by partner, listing is complete.
Days 11 to 14: UAT testing
Partner Ops conducts user acceptance testing on all systems. 100% pass rate required before proceeding.

Pre-book onboarding (runs alongside standard onboarding)

Who does what
Partner Ops (Abel) handles availability configuration. Product (Taz, Faz) handles booking system integration. Product (Loriana) handles LMS portal setup. The Partnership Manager ensures handoffs are clean and monitors progress via Salesforce.
Adding pre-book to an existing lounge
If you are adding pre-book to a lounge that is already live on the network, the standard lounge setup steps do not apply. Follow only the pre-book configuration steps below.
Week 1: Availability configuration
Partner Ops (Abel) creates the lounge resource, configures the pre-agreed availability schedule from the form, sets slot patterns and capacity limits, and provides environment IDs for testing.

If LMS Pre-Book Portal or Full LMS Suite: Product (Loriana) creates the partner's LMS account and configures the lounge in the portal. Partner Ops emails Loriana when the form shows LMS is required (manual step for now).

If email only: Partner Ops configures the reservation email field in the outlet record.

Note: The LMS team does not have access to the outlet record. Partner Ops handles that configuration and notifies the LMS team when LMS setup is needed.
Weeks 2 to 4: Integration and testing
Product (Taz and Faz) runs booking system integration: loads parameters (booking window, cancellation window, maximum group size, pricing), runs end-to-end testing covering availability, booking, and confirmation delivery. 100% pass rate required.
Week 4: Partner training and readiness
If LMS Pre-Book Portal or Full LMS Suite: train partner staff on viewing reservations, confirming guest arrivals, and using reports for invoicing.

If email only: explain how email confirmations work, confirm partner knows how to track reservations manually.

All variants: confirm the partner can receive and manage pre-bookings. Ensure the partner knows how to reach support for pre-book issues.

Weekly milestones

Days 1 to 7: Launch validation
Partner goes live and processes first transactions. Partner Ops monitors technical performance. Review listing in web and app to confirm outlet appears correctly, in the right category, with current information. Week 1 check-in call covers operational readiness and any issues.
Days 8 to 14: Volume and feedback
Transaction volume tracking begins. Staff and guest feedback collection. Week 2 check-in call covers volume trends and operational adjustments. Handoff from Partner Ops to Partnership Manager begins.
Days 15 to 21: Invoice support
PM now owns the relationship. First invoice preparation support. Finance process validation. Week 3 check-in call covers invoice readiness and any questions.
Days 22 to 30: Health check and transition
30-day health check meeting: performance review and satisfaction assessment. Health score assigned (Green / Yellow / Red). Transition to standard QBR rhythm. Handoff to ongoing management complete.

Pre-book activation (additional monitoring)

Pre-book activation runs on the same 30-day timeline as standard activation, with extra checks.

Days 1 to 3: First pre-bookings
Confirm first pre-bookings are created successfully. Confirm the partner is receiving notifications (email or portal). Verify capacity limits are working correctly.
Days 4 to 7: Early review
Review early pre-booking patterns. Collect initial partner feedback. Address any issues.
Days 8 to 30: Ongoing monitoring
Review pre-booking volumes over time. Assess if the pre-agreed capacity allocation is appropriate. Collect partner feedback on the operational experience. Include pre-book in the 30-day health check. Transition pre-book support to standard channels.

Volume will vary. Some lounges may not see their first pre-bookings for several days or weeks, depending on how actively the service is promoted. Adjust your monitoring accordingly.
Day 14 handoff
Product hands off pre-book monitoring to the Partnership Manager at Day 14. From this point, include pre-book status in your weekly check-ins and the 30-day health check.

Exception governance

The onboarding process is designed to ensure quality and readiness. Exceptions require formal approval and documented business justification.

Standard process (no exceptions)

  • Contract must be signed before onboarding case creation
  • LIF must be complete before the onboarding clock starts
  • Standard timeline is 21 business days from contract signature to launch readiness
  • All stage gate criteria must be met before proceeding to the next stage

Escalation: request to proceed without a contract

If Partner Operations is asked to proceed with onboarding before the contract is signed:

  1. Partner Ops declines and documents the request in the Salesforce case
  2. Partner Ops escalates to Regional Partnership Director/VP
  3. Regional Partnership Director/VP resolves with appropriate stakeholders before any work proceeds
  4. If an exception is absolutely required (extremely rare, given legal and financial risk), final approval sits with SVP Global Partnerships
Standard answer
No onboarding work begins without a signed contract. This protects Collinson from financial exposure and legal liability.

Exception types

Type Definition Approver Conditions
A: Accelerated timeline Complete onboarding in fewer than 21 business days Regional Partnership Director Tier 1A/1B strategic partner. Business justification documented (competitive threat, client requirement). Partner Ops confirms technical feasibility. Contract and LIF still must be complete. UAT cannot be skipped.
B: Partial information start Begin onboarding with incomplete LIF Partner Ops Lead 90% of LIF complete. Missing fields are non-critical TBC. Clear deadline set. Clock stops until information complete. UAT pauses until fields received.
C: Launch without PCI Activate device before PCI compliance form is signed VP Global Partnerships Urgent business requirement documented. Partner commits to completion within 10 days of launch. PCI form sent the same day as device kit. Escalation ladder: Day 10 documented reminder, Day 20 escalation to partner senior leadership, Day 30 escalation to VP Global Partnerships.

Pre-book exceptions

Scenario Action
Partner wants pre-book but cannot commit to pre-agreed availability Explain that pre-agreed capacity is required. Without it, we cannot manage availability or prevent overbooking. Escalate to Product if the partner has an alternative technical approach. Do not proceed with this process.
Non-standard request (e.g. cancellation window below 48 hours, unusual capacity patterns) Document in Section 5 of the Pre-Book Onboarding Form. Escalate to Product (Taz and Faz) before the contract is signed. Product provides go/no-go and timeline impact. Do not promise non-standard terms without Product approval.
Accelerated pre-book timeline (fewer than 28 business days) Follow standard exception governance. Regional Partnership Director approval + Product feasibility confirmation. Minimum 14 business days due to technical dependencies.

Never approved

These exceptions create unacceptable risk and will not be approved under any circumstances.

  • Shipping devices before contract signed: creates financial exposure and legal liability
  • Starting onboarding without postal codes (where required): causes logistics failure
  • Starting onboarding without banking details: prevents invoice processing
  • Skipping UAT testing: creates operational failure risk
  • Launching before training complete: partner and staff not trained to serve guests

Pre-book variants guide

When a partner agrees to pre-book with pre-agreed availability, they choose one of three management variants.

Variant What the partner gets Best fit
Email only A confirmation email for every pre-booking. No portal access. Low-volume partners, or partners who do not expect frequent pre-bookings. Harder to manage at scale.
LMS Pre-Book Portal (recommended) Portal to view upcoming reservations, confirm guest arrivals, and access reports. Plus email confirmations. Recommended for most partners.
Full LMS Suite Everything in the portal, plus full lounge management: check-in, waitlist, and comprehensive reporting. Partners who want to manage all lounge operations through one system.
Full LMS Suite
Partners choosing Full LMS Suite use a different onboarding form and a different configuration process. Contact Loriana Martinez (Product) if a partner selects this option.

Key principles for pre-book

  • A confirmed booking means confirmed access. Partners commit to honouring the slots they allocate. A pre-booked member has a reservation, not a waitlist position.
  • LMS Portal is optional but recommended. Email only is difficult to manage at scale. Encourage the LMS Pre-Book Portal for any partner expecting regular volume.
  • Do not promise anything non-standard without Product approval. Cancellation windows below 48 hours, non-standard capacity patterns, or anything outside the norm must go to Taz (Product) before the contract is signed.
  • The form must be complete before handoff. An incomplete handoff causes delays for Partner Operations and Product. Do not send a partial form.

Schedule changes (post-launch)

After launch, partners may need to update their availability: capacity changes, new blackout dates, and so on.

Process

  1. Partner contacts their Partnership Manager with the requested change
  2. Partner resubmits the availability section of the Pre-Book Onboarding Form with the changes marked
  3. Partnership Manager (or Wendy) assigns the update task to Partner Operations
  4. Partner Operations updates the configuration
  5. Partner Operations confirms completion
  6. Partnership Manager notifies the partner

Schedule changes take approximately 2 to 3 business days.

Policy changes take longer
Changes to booking policy (cancellation window, booking window, maximum group size) involve the Product team. Coordinate with Taz early.
